Could the ammonia in this experiment be explained by the contamination that was actually measured?

Detecting ammonia does not tell you where its nitrogen came from. NitroTrace converts an experiment's product measurement and its contamination evidence — delivered-gas impurities, matched blanks and component-release tests — to one basis (nmol N), builds a conservative contamination budget, and shows which sources remain unknown. Missing measurements stay unknown, never zero; overlapping blanks are not summed; and every input keeps its source, page and quote.

A software prototype for reviewing interpretation. It does not replace a laboratory investigation, and its results are not judgements of the original studies.
